## Platform

## Product Purpose
KeepScope protects personal data in text before it is sent to AI models. Applications choose authorized policy profiles. A person reviews protected content and destination and approves that exact revision before sending. Missing conditions block the request.

## Users
Enterprise teams evaluating data protection for AI. Source: existing landing copy, retained in the user-approved brand/content direction. Specific buyer persona is unconfirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Detection covers names, Chilean RUT, phones, addresses and email. Human review can correct missed or false detections. Policy may retain, omit or replace data, preserving text outside authorized transformations. Sending revalidates authorization and uses permitted routes. Audit records contain rules, versions and transitions without content. Records have limited retention; after content expires they cannot reconstruct it. JSONL audit export uses a restricted operational tool.
Restitution checks response format, tokens and privacy. The client validates results and requests only permitted fields before expiry with current permissions. Detection and encrypted vault run in customer infrastructure; external model destinations each require authorization. API integration. No claim of guaranteed detection, certification or legal compliance.
